How it works

What it checks

  • HTTPS and certificateValid, trusted, matching the hostname, and not expired.
  • HTTP to HTTPS redirectPlain HTTP visitors are sent to the secure site.
  • Security headersHSTS, CSP, Referrer-Policy and three more.
  • CookiesSecure, HttpOnly and SameSite flags.
  • Information disclosureServer and X-Powered-By version details.

Built to stay safe

  • No attacksOnly ordinary GET requests, with timeouts and limits.
  • SSRF protectionEvery address and redirect is validated before connecting.
  • Certificates always verifiedTLS checking is never switched off.
  • Honest scoringA score out of 100 for configuration only. It is not a full safety rating.
